## Why this matters

Milestone 1 (`docs/PRODUCT_VISION.md:532-543`) requires "at least 12 high-intent
pages" answering distinct Chilean PC-purchase questions, with unique evidence,
freshness, canonicalization, and internal linking, on an information
architecture "outside a single opaque SPA state". Plan 033 deliberately builds
only a three-page authored pilot and states that expansion to twelve pages
"requires observed unique query value, indexing evidence, maintained freshness,
and qualified Analyzer starts" (`plans/033-build-crawlable-decision-content-foundation.md:30-31,46-47`),
and `plans/README.md:142-145,242-244` repeats that the remaining pages are
gated and that immediate programmatic/mass content is rejected.

What is missing is the artifact that makes the gated expansion cheap and safe:
a rule-backed inventory for pages 4-12 that ties each candidate to an Analyzer
rule ID, the Plan 030 coverage state for that rule, and the observation
evidence that unlocks it. This spike produces that inventory. It does not
author pages, does not touch the SPA, and does not authorize deployment.

## Steps

### Step 1: Extract the rule and coverage facts

1. List the conformance rule IDs with the command above and record them.
2. Extract the per-rule assessability from
   `data/processed/assessment-coverage.min.json`. For each rule, note the
   assessable/total combination counts and classify coverage as high/medium/
   low/absent using explicit thresholds you state in the document (e.g.
   assessable ≥ 80% high, ≥ 50% medium, > 0 low, 0 absent).
3. Verify that every rule ID you plan to cite exists in
   `ASSURANCE_RULE_IDS`; if the manifest uses keys that are not rule IDs,
   report the mismatch instead of guessing.

If `data/processed/assessment-coverage.min.json` is missing or malformed, STOP
and report (do not regenerate data in this plan).

### Step 2: Write `docs/design/search-content-inventory.md`

Required sections (done criteria check them):

1. `## Objetivo y límites` — what this inventory is for, and that it
   authorizes no page authoring, no deployment, no sitemap submission, and no
   programmatic generation.
2. `## Criterios de elegibilidad` — the per-page bar: one distinct user
   decision, at least one supported Analyzer rule with evidence, plan-030
   coverage state recorded, a named CTA into the Analyzer, and an observation
   gate inherited from Plan 033.
3. `## Fuentes de reglas y cobertura` — the rule-ID source command and the
   coverage-state source, with the thresholds used and their caveats.
4. `## Inventario de páginas 4-12` — a table with exactly 9 candidate rows
   (pages 4 through 12), one per distinct Chilean gaming-PC purchase question.
   Columns: `#`, `Intención de búsqueda`, `Pregunta de decisión`,
   `Reglas evaluadas (IDs)`, `Estado de cobertura Plan 030`,
   `Evidencia y frescura requeridas`, `Enlaces internos`, `CTA al Analyzer`,
   `Evidencia de observación que la desbloquea`.
   The last column stays empty until Plan 033's pilot produces observation
   data; state that explicitly in a note under the table.
5. `## Libro de expansión` — a ledger template (expand / maintain / retire)
   keyed to observed indexing, query, and qualified-Analyzer-start evidence,
   including who decides and how often it is reviewed.
6. `## Enlazado y canonical` — how the 12 pages interlink and link to the
   product workflow, consistent with the SPA's routing constraints (no router
   today; note the open architectural question without solving it).
7. `## Riesgos y sesgos` — thin/duplicated content risk, coverage overclaim
   risk (a page must not claim a rule the coverage manifest marks absent),
   freshness drift.
8. `## Decisiones pendientes del propietario` — topic priority, the
   rule-coverage floor per page, and confirmation that expansion stays
   observation-gated.
9. `## No objetivos` — the explicit rejections: building the nine pages now,
   programmatic SEO, external Search Console/sitemap actions, touching
   `docs/PRODUCT_VISION.md`.

### Step 3: Self-check the inventory

For each of the 9 rows: at least one rule ID exists in `ASSURANCE_RULE_IDS`,
the coverage state matches the manifest classification, and the page's
decision question is distinct from the other rows (no near-duplicates). If a
candidate has no supported rule, replace it or mark it `(deferida)` with the
reason — do not pad the table with unsupported topics.

**Verify**: `rg -n "^## " docs/design/search-content-inventory.md` lists the
nine headings; the table has 9 data rows — count with
`rg -c '^\| (4|5|6|7|8|9|10|11|12) \|' docs/design/search-content-inventory.md`
and state the count in your report.
